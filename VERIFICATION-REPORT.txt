Important limitation:
The app is an offline deterministic fuzzy engine, not a hosted generative AI model. It cannot honestly guarantee 100% semantic accuracy for every possible Russian word or sentence, especially a completely unrelated or extremely ambiguous string. The implemented behavior is designed to recover strongly damaged words when the intended word exists in the local vocabulary/context.
